Questions, answered

How does a project start?

Send a short brief using the form above or by email. I reply within a couple of working days to set up a call, then follow up with a proposal covering scope, timeline and cost.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A focused identity or landing page usually takes a few weeks; larger systems and products take longer. You get a clear timeline before anything starts.

Do you work with clients outside Nepal?

Yes. I am based in Kathmandu and work remotely with clients in any time zone, with regular check-ins over video calls.

What do you need from me?

A clear idea of your goals, your audience and any existing material — brand assets, references or content. Honest feedback along the way makes the work better.
