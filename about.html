(About)Designer — Kathmandu

Hello, I'm Rojina

A multidisciplinary designer working across brand identity, digital products and motion — for people who want their ideas to look as good as they are.

My story ↓
(01)Story

Design, for me, starts with listening. The best ideas are usually already in the room — my job is to find them, shape them and give them a voice people remember.

I work with startups, cultural organisations and growing brands, moving between strategy, typography, interfaces and motion. Each project gets the same care whether it is a single logo or a full product.

Outside client work I explore 3D, type experiments and illustration. Those side projects keep my eye sharp and often find their way back into the work.

(02)Approach

How a project comes together

01

Listen

Every project starts with questions: who it is for, what needs to change and what success looks like. I learn your world before drawing anything.

02

Define

Research turns into a clear direction — a strategy, a moodboard and a few honest routes we can react to together.

03

Make

The chosen route grows into the real thing: identity systems, screens, motion. Shared early and often, so there are no surprises.

04

Refine

Details get polished, files get organised and guidelines get written, so the work keeps looking right long after hand-off.
